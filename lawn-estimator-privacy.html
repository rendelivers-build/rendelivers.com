Privacy Policy — Lawn Estimator App

Last updated: October 6, 2026

We collect nothing ourselves. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no contact forms in the app — we never see your name, email, or phone number. Everything the app does with your data happens on your phone or with Google's services:

  • Addresses you type in are sent to Google Maps/Places so the app can find the property and show satellite imagery. We don't run any servers, so we don't keep a copy — your searches stay between your phone and Google.
  • Your location: the map can show your current location (the blue dot) so you can find the property you're quoting. That stays on your phone — we don't collect it, store it, or send it anywhere.
  • Estimates and app settings are stored on your device only (and in your device's backup if Android backup is on). We can't see them and we can't recover them.
  • Free-version ads: the free tier shows ads through Google AdMob. Google's privacy policy covers how they handle ad data — upgrading to Lawn Estimator Pro removes ads.
  • Pro subscriptions: payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never touch your payment info.

Third-party services involved: Google Maps/Places, Google AdMob, Google Play Billing. Their privacy policies govern their data handling.
