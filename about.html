Who's Ren?

Ren's the guy who's spent 30+ years on job sites — ground to finish, seven states, more busted knuckles than he can count. He's run crews, run companies, and learned the hard way which tools earn their keep and which ones end up in the bottom of the gang box.

This site is everything he wishes someone had handed him on day one: the tools that actually work, the safety gear that gets you home in one piece, the templates that keep the paperwork from eating your weekends, and shirts that say what every tradie's thinking.

Why This Site Exists

Because most tool recommendations come from people who've never swung a hammer. Every product here is hand-picked and individually priced by someone who's actually used this stuff — no algorithms, no auto-generated lists. If it can't survive a real job site, it doesn't make the list.

No fluff. No sponsored placements. Just the stuff that works.
